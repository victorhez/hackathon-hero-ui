1. Product Overview
ClearLend is an identity-powered DeFi lending platform built on the Cleanverse compliance infrastructure. It uses
Cleanverse Verified Identity (CVI / A-Pass) and Cleanverse Verified Assets (CVA / A-Token) to create a gated
lending pool where verified users can borrow and lend with better terms than traditional over-collateralised DeFi —
simply by proving who they are.
The core innovation: in standard DeFi, you need to lock up more money than you borrow. ClearLend changes this
by treating your verified identity and clean on-chain history as a financial signal — reducing how much collateral
you need and unlocking better interest rates. The cleaner your identity and transaction history, the better your
borrowing terms.

2. The Problem Being Solved
DeFi lending has a fundamental design flaw: it excludes the people who need it most. To borrow $1,000 in
standard DeFi, you must lock up $1,500 first. This means only people who already have crypto wealth can
participate — a system that reinforces inequality rather than opening access. Meanwhile, the real financial system
uses identity and credit history to determine trustworthiness. DeFi ignores both.

3. The Solution — How ClearLend Works
ClearLend gates access to its lending pool using Cleanverse's CVI. Only bank-verified, A-Pass-holding users can
enter. Once inside, the system reads their on-chain transaction history (via CVA / A-Token records) to compute a
Reputation Score — and that score directly determines their borrowing tier, collateral requirements, and interest
rate.
Step 1 — Connect User connects their Web3 wallet to ClearLend. The platform checks for a valid CVI
(A-Pass) credential issued by Cleanverse.
Step 2 — Gate Check If CVI is present: user enters the platform. If not: they are directed to Cleanverse's
one-time verification flow (bank credential + wallet binding) to obtain their A-Pass.
Step 3 — Score ClearLend reads the user's CVA transaction history — the volume, frequency, and
cleanliness of their A-Token transfers — and computes a Reputation Score (0–100).
Step 4 — Tier The Reputation Score maps the user to a borrowing tier (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze,
or Unranked), which determines collateral ratio, borrowing limit, and interest rate.
Step 5 — Borrow/Lend Borrowers select a loan amount within their tier limit, deposit the required collateral (in
A-Tokens), and receive funds. Lenders deposit A-Tokens into the pool to earn yield.
Step 6 — Repay Borrowers repay principal + interest over the loan term. On-time repayment improves
their Reputation Score over time, unlocking better tiers.
4. Reputation Score & Borrowing Tier System
The Reputation Score is the heart of ClearLend. It is a number from 0 to 100 that reflects how trustworthy a verified
user appears, based on their on-chain behaviour within the Cleanverse ecosystem. It is not a static number — it
grows or shrinks based on the user's actions over time.
Score Range Tier Collateral Required Borrowing Limit Interest Rate
85 – 100 ■ Platinum 0% – 20% Up to $50,000 Lowest (Prime)
65 – 84 ■ Gold 25% – 40% Up to $20,000 Competitive
45 – 64 ■ Silver 50% – 70% Up to $10,000 Standard
20 – 44 ■ Bronze 80% – 100% Up to $2,000 Higher
0 – 19 ■ Unranked 120%+ Micro only Maximum
New Cleanverse users start at a baseline score. Every on-time repayment, clean transfer, and active verified transaction adds
points. Late repayments, defaults, or suspicious patterns reduce it.
5. What ClearLend Analyses — The Four Score Dimensions
1. CVI Verification Level Is the user's A-Pass valid and active? A freshly issued or recently renewed A-Pass
scores higher. Expired or unverified credentials result in access denial until
re-verified.
2. CVA Transaction History How many A-Token (clean, compliant) transfers has this wallet made? Volume,
consistency, and the duration of activity all feed into the score. Longer, cleaner history
= higher score.
3. Loan Repayment Record ClearLend's own internal record: has this user borrowed before? Did they repay on
time? Every on-time repayment boosts the score. Defaults are heavily penalised.
4. Wallet Age & Activity How long has this wallet been active on Cleanverse? An older, consistently active
wallet demonstrates commitment to the ecosystem and scores more favourably than
a brand-new wallet.
6. Full Feature List
6.1 Identity Gate & Onboarding
• Wallet connection via MetaMask, WalletConnect, or Coinbase Wallet
• Automatic CVI (A-Pass) check on login — unverified users are redirected to Cleanverse verification
• One-time verification flow embedded or linked: user completes bank credential binding on Cleanverse, returns

6.2 Reputation Score Dashboard
• Visual score display (0–100) shown prominently on the user's home screen
• Current tier badge: Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze / Unranked — colour-coded and clearly labelled
• Score breakdown: shows the contribution of each of the four scoring dimensions
• Score history chart: shows how the user's score has changed over time
• Next tier progress bar: shows exactly what the user needs to do to reach the next tier
• Plain-language tips: 'Repay your next loan on time to gain +8 points' — actionable, not abstract
6.3 Borrowing Flow
• Loan calculator: user inputs desired amount, sees collateral required and interest rate in real time based on
their tier
• Loan term selection: 7 days, 14 days, or 30 days
• Collateral deposit in A-Tokens — compliant, traced, and locked in the smart contract for the loan term
• Loan confirmation screen with full breakdown: amount, term, rate, collateral, total repayment
• Active loan tracker: shows outstanding balance, time remaining, next repayment date, and penalty if late
• One-click repayment button — user repays directly from their A-Token wallet
• Automatic collateral release upon full repayment
• Late payment warning notifications (in-app) before the due date
6.4 Lending / Liquidity Pool
• Lenders deposit A-Tokens into the ClearLend pool to earn yield
• Real-time pool stats: total liquidity, current utilisation rate, APY for lenders
• Lender dashboard: shows deposited amount, earned interest to date, and withdrawal option
• Only CVI-verified wallets can deposit as lenders — maintaining the compliance gate on both sides
• Yield distributed pro-rata from borrower interest payments
• Minimum deposit: low barrier to encourage participation from small lenders
6.5 Compliance & Cleanverse Integration
• All transactions settled in CVA (A-Tokens) — ensuring Travel Rule compliance on every loan and repayment
• CVI credential verified on every login and before every loan — not just at onboarding
• On-chain proof generated for every loan event (issuance, repayment, default) — fully auditable
• Sanctions and blacklist enforcement inherited from Cleanverse's on-chain policy engine
• No anonymous wallets can access any part of the platform — zero exceptions
• Audit log: every action (deposit, borrow, repay, withdraw) is logged and traceable on-chain
6.6 Smart Contract Layer
• Lending pool contract: holds lender deposits, disburses loans, collects repayments
• Collateral vault contract: locks borrower collateral for the loan term, releases on repayment
• Reputation oracle contract: reads CVI status and CVA history to compute and update scores
• Liquidation logic: if a borrower defaults past the grace period, collateral is liquidated to cover lenders
• All contracts open-source and auditable — required for hackathon submission
• Deployed on testnet for hackathon demo; architecture supports mainnet deployment on any
Cleanverse-integrated chain
6.7 Notifications & User Alerts
• In-app notifications: loan due in 48 hours, score changed, tier upgraded, repayment confirmed
• Score milestone alerts: 'You've reached Gold tier — your next loan gets a lower rate'
• Tier downgrade warning: 'Your score dropped — take action to avoid losing your tier'
• Lender alerts: 'Your deposit has earned X USDC this week'

8. Cleanverse Technical Requirements
The following Cleanverse components must be integrated for a valid hackathon submission. Both CVI and CVA
must be actively used — not just referenced.
CVI — Cleanverse Verified Identity (A-Pass)
Gate all platform access behind a valid A-Pass check — no CVI, no entry
Re-verify CVI status on each login session, not just at signup
Display the user's verification status visibly on their dashboard
Use A-Pass credential data as an input to the Reputation Score calculation
Redirect unverified users to Cleanverse's one-time bank verification flow
CVA — Cleanverse Verified Assets (A-Token)
All loan disbursements, collateral deposits, repayments, and lender yields must settle in A-Tokens
A-Token transaction history must be read to compute the CVA dimension of the Reputation Score
No non-A-Token assets accepted anywhere in the lending flow
A-Token transfer proofs used as on-chain audit trail for every loan event
Demonstrates Travel Rule compliance on every transaction — a core Cleanverse requirement
